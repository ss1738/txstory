# Participant information (read or hand to the participant)

**What this is.** We are comparing two websites that show what happened in a
blockchain transaction. We are evaluating the websites, not you.

**What you'll do.** For about 35 to 40 minutes you will look at 10 real, public
transactions: 5 on one website, then 5 on the other. You'll answer a few questions
about each, out loud.

**What we record.** Your answers, how long each one takes, how confident you felt
(1 to 5), how familiar you are with blockchain explorers (1 to 5), and short notes. We use
a code (like P03) instead of your name.

**What we don't record.** No name, email, wallet address, balances, screen
recording, audio, or analytics. You won't be asked to connect a wallet or sign
anything.

**Your choices.** You can skip any question or stop at any time, with no need to
explain. You can ask for your answers to be deleted afterwards. They are kept in
one local file, which can simply be deleted.

**Rules during the tasks.** Please use only the website you're given (any page on
it is fine). Don't use search engines or AI assistants. The facilitator can't help
with the answers. "I don't know" is a perfectly good answer.
